Research-only Exotic Longshot Intrusion Phase 7 request.

Context:
- Phase 1/2 established that runner-vs-field features improve horse-level Top-3
  probability, including the pre-registered historical-final-win-odds >= 6.0
  longshot proxy.
- Phase 3-5 failed to convert those marginal probabilities into robust exotic
  combination probabilities for longshot-containing outcomes.
- Phase 6 direct three-runner set ranking failed decisively versus Phase 5.
- Do not retune failed Phase 3-6 transforms or lower their gates.

Phase 7 structural hypothesis:
Model the first hard subproblem directly: among unpopular runners, identify
which one is most likely to intrude into the top three.

Baseline:
- General Phase 2-style Top-3 challenger.
- Train on all eligible runners through 2022-12-31.
- Existing v10 history, market context, and runner-vs-field interaction features.

Specialist:
- Same fixed CatBoost classifier family/settings.
- Train only on the pre-registered development proxy:
  historical final win odds >= 6.0 through 2022-12-31.
- Add fixed intrusion features comparing each candidate continuously against:
  - the market favorite;
  - the market top-three mean;
  for past-only horse form, early/late running-style history, jockey history,
  horse-jockey history, trainer history, plus market rank/probability position.
- These features must not depend on same-race finish outcomes.

Evaluation:
- Reused development years 2023 and 2024 separately.
- Evaluate only the same historical-final-win-odds >= 6.0 development proxy.
- Compare specialist versus general baseline on:
  1. binary Top-3 log-loss;
  2. Brier score;
  3. top-longshot selection hit rate: in each race, choose the longshot candidate
     with the highest predicted Top-3 probability and test whether it actually
     finished first/second/third.
- Deterministic paired race bootstrap:
  1000 samples, seed 20261006.

Pre-registered Phase 7 gate:
For BOTH 2023 and 2024:
- specialist-minus-baseline log-loss < 0;
- specialist-minus-baseline Brier < 0;
- specialist top-pick hit-rate delta > 0;
- bootstrap support for lower log-loss >= 80%;
- bootstrap support for lower Brier >= 80%;
- bootstrap support for higher top-pick hit rate >= 80%.

If Phase 7 fails, do not lower thresholds or tune on 2023/2024. Move to a new
structural hypothesis.

Boundaries:
- Historical final win odds are a development-only proxy; this phase does not
  claim deployable pre-race longshot identification until a timestamped lawful
  pre-race market source is verified.
- No trio/trifecta EV claim, no real ticket generation, no staking changes.
- 2025-2026 final independent holdout remain untouched and unread.
- Forward Paper remains unchanged.
- No paid tool, API, or data source may be used.
