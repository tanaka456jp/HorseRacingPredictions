Research-only Exotic Direct Trio Phase 6 request.

Context:
- Phase 1/2 showed that runner-vs-field features improve horse-level Top-3
  probability, including the pre-registered longshot proxy.
- Phase 3, Phase 4, and Phase 5 failed their pre-registered gates when those
  horse-level marginals were transformed into three-runner combination
  probabilities.
- Do not retune any failed transform or lower its support threshold.

Phase 6 structural hypothesis:
Train a race-group ranking model directly on unordered three-runner sets rather
than converting horse-level marginals into a set probability.

Training design:
- Source data: existing leakage-safe pre-race v10 history features plus current
  race market context and runner-vs-field interaction features.
- Train through 2022-12-31 only.
- For each training race, keep exactly one positive set: the actual first,
  second, and third finishers.
- Cap negatives at 31 per race, fixed before evaluation:
  - up to 8 highest market-product hard negatives;
  - up to 12 negatives containing a pre-registered longshot proxy;
  - fill the remaining slots by deterministic random sampling.
- Seed: 20261006.
- Direct set model: CatBoostRanker YetiRankPairwise, 250 iterations, depth 7,
  learning rate 0.05. Do not tune these settings from 2023/2024 evidence.
- Candidate set representation is permutation invariant by sorting the three
  members by market probability before slotting features, with fixed set-level
  aggregates for market, recent Top-3 form, running-style history, jockey,
  trainer, and horse-jockey history.

Evaluation:
- Enumerate ALL three-runner sets in each reused development race.
- Normalize direct model scores across all sets in each race.
- Compare the probability assigned to the realized unordered top-three set with
  the frozen Phase 5 exact-three probability produced from the frozen Phase 2
  Top-3 challenger.
- Evaluate 2023 and 2024 separately.
- Evaluate both all races and races whose realized top three includes at least
  one runner with historical final win odds >= 6.0.
- Metric: realized trio-set negative log likelihood.
- Paired race bootstrap: 1000 samples, seed 20261006.
- Phase 6 passes only if direct-minus-Phase5 NLL < 0 and bootstrap improvement
  support >= 80% in BOTH overall and longshot-containing segments in BOTH
  2023 and 2024.

Boundaries:
- This is trio set probability research only. It does not estimate betting EV.
- Historical final win odds are development-only market proxies.
- No real ticket generation or staking change.
- 2025-2026 final independent holdout must remain untouched and unread.
- Forward Paper remains unchanged.
- No paid tool, API, or data source may be used.
- If Phase 6 fails, do not lower thresholds or tune on 2023/2024; move to a new
  structural hypothesis.
