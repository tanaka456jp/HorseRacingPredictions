Research-only Exotic Longshot Residual Phase 9 request.

Context:
- Phase 7 specialist worsened Top-3 log-loss/Brier but improved top-longshot
  selection hit rate in both reused development years.
- Phase 8 direct longshot ranking objective did not reproduce that signal.
- Do not tune Phase 7/8 thresholds or model parameters on 2023/2024.

Phase 9 structural hypothesis:
Treat the longshot signal as a residual around the calibrated general Top-3
model rather than as a standalone classifier or ranker.

Stacking-safety design:
- General Top-3 baseline uses the frozen Phase 2-style feature/model family.
- Build residual training examples only from annual out-of-fold predictions:
  - 2018 validation: baseline trained only on dates before 2018-01-01;
  - 2019 validation: baseline trained only on dates before 2019-01-01;
  - 2020 validation: baseline trained only on dates before 2020-01-01;
  - 2021 validation: baseline trained only on dates before 2021-01-01;
  - 2022 validation: baseline trained only on dates before 2022-01-01.
- Residual target for a longshot candidate is:
  actual_top3_indicator - annual_OOF_general_top3_probability.
- Candidate regime remains historical final win odds >= 6.0, development proxy.
- No same-row in-sample baseline prediction may be used to create a residual
  training target.

Residual model:
- CatBoostRegressor with RMSE.
- Fixed before evaluation: 250 iterations, depth 6, learning rate 0.03,
  random seed 42, l2_leaf_reg 8.
- Inputs are the Phase 7 leakage-safe specialist features plus fixed baseline
  overlay features:
  general Top-3 probability, general-minus-market probability, general/market
  ratio, general probability minus longshot-race mean, and within-race
  longshot general-probability rank percentile.
- Predicted residual is clipped to [-0.5, 0.5].
- Corrected probability is fixed as:
  clip(general_top3_probability + predicted_residual, 1e-6, 1-1e-6).
- No blend weight, calibration parameter, threshold, or odds band is tuned.

Evaluation:
- Final general baseline is trained through 2022-12-31.
- Reused development years 2023 and 2024 are evaluated separately.
- Longshot proxy candidates only.
- Compare corrected versus general baseline on:
  1. binary Top-3 log-loss;
  2. Brier score;
  3. per-race top1 longshot Top-3 hit rate;
  4. mean reciprocal rank of first actual Top-3 longshot.
- Paired race bootstrap: 1000 samples, seed 20261006.

Pre-registered gate, required in BOTH 2023 and 2024:
- log-loss delta < 0 and bootstrap support >= 80%;
- Brier delta < 0 and bootstrap support >= 80%;
- top1 hit-rate delta > 0 and bootstrap support >= 80%;
- MRR delta > 0 and bootstrap support >= 80%.

If Phase 9 fails, do not tune residual scale or thresholds on 2023/2024. Use the
failure pattern to choose a new structural hypothesis.

Boundaries:
- Historical final win odds remain a development-only proxy.
- No trio/trifecta EV claim, no real ticket generation, no staking changes.
- 2025-2026 final independent holdout remain untouched and unread.
- Forward Paper remains unchanged.
- No paid tool, API, or data source may be used.
