Research-only Exotic / Top-3 Phase 1 request.

Hypothesis:
Popular/non-popular runners may be mispriced for place-in-the-top-three outcomes
when past-only horse form, jockey history, running-style context, and the
composition of the current field are evaluated jointly rather than runner by
runner.

Design:
- Build a baseline Top-3 probability model using the existing pre-race v10
  history features plus existing win-market context.
- Build one challenger with fixed runner-vs-field interaction features:
  within-race means, deltas, dispersion, ranks, and market concentration.
- Use the same fixed CatBoost classifier settings for baseline and challenger.
- Train through 2022-12-31.
- Evaluate unchanged on reused development years 2023 and 2024.
- Report both overall Top-3 binary log-loss/Brier and the pre-registered
  historical-final-win-odds >= 6.0 longshot proxy.
- The development gate passes only if challenger binary log-loss and Brier are
  both lower than baseline overall AND in the longshot proxy in BOTH 2023 and
  2024.
- Do not tune a probability threshold, odds band, feature threshold, or staking
  rule in this phase.

Pedigree:
The current free history exposes a blood registration identifier but not
semantic sire/dam/broodmare-sire fields. Do not use the identifier as a
categorical memorization proxy. Pedigree features require a separately verified
free/legal semantic source before they may enter a later research phase.

Boundaries:
- 2023/2024 are reused development evidence, not independent holdout.
- 2025-2026 remain untouched as the final independent holdout.
- Do not read or evaluate 2025-2026.
- Forward Paper settings remain unchanged.
- Do not create or recommend real trifecta/trio tickets in this phase.
- Current free pipeline has no pre-race exotic-combination odds, so this phase
  tests probability quality only, not exotic-bet expected value.
- No paid tool, API, or data source may be used.

Phase 2 safety validation:
Freeze the Phase 1 baseline/challenger feature sets and CatBoost settings.
Do not add, remove, or tune features, thresholds, odds bands, or model
hyperparameters from Phase 2 evidence.

For each reused development year (2023 and 2024), run a deterministic paired
race bootstrap comparing challenger minus baseline Top-3 binary log-loss and
Brier:
- 1000 bootstrap samples
- seed 20261006
- resample races with replacement
- evaluate both the full field and the already pre-registered historical-final-
  win-odds >= 6.0 longshot proxy
- require at least 80% bootstrap support for challenger improvement in BOTH
  binary log-loss and Brier, for BOTH full-field and longshot evidence, in BOTH
  2023 and 2024

This Phase 2 gate is uncertainty validation only. It is not a new tuning
objective and it does not authorize trifecta/trio selection or Forward Paper
changes. 2025-2026 remain untouched. No paid tool/API/data source may be used.

Phase 3 exotic-combination probability validation:
Freeze the Phase 2 Top-3 baseline/challenger models, features, hyperparameters,
longshot boundary, bootstrap sample count, seed, and support threshold.
Do not retrain differently, tune another threshold, or select any betting rule
from Phase 3 evidence.

Convert each frozen horse-level Top-3 marginal probability q to a fixed
Plackett-Luce ranking strength q/(1-q). For every completed development race
with unique first/second/third finishers:
- ordered top-three probability is the Plackett-Luce probability of the actual
  1st->2nd->3rd sequence (trifecta probability proxy);
- unordered top-three-set probability is the sum of the six permutations of
  those same three actual horses (trio probability proxy).
No parameter is fitted in this conversion.

Evaluate unchanged on reused development years 2023 and 2024:
- mean negative log likelihood of the realized ordered top three;
- mean negative log likelihood of the realized unordered top-three set;
- the same two metrics for races whose realized top three contains at least one
  pre-registered longshot proxy (historical final win odds >= 6.0);
- deterministic paired race bootstrap, 1000 samples, seed 20261006.

Phase 3 passes only if challenger-minus-baseline NLL is below zero AND bootstrap
support for lower NLL is at least 80% for BOTH trifecta and trio probability
proxies, BOTH overall and longshot-containing races, in BOTH 2023 and 2024.

This is probability-quality research only. It does not establish betting EV
because the current free pipeline does not contain timestamped pre-race
trifecta/trio combination odds. Do not generate real tickets or change staking.
2025-2026 remain untouched as final independent holdout. Forward Paper remains
unchanged. No paid tool, API, or data source may be used.

Phase 4 role-aware exotic-combination hypothesis:
Phase 3 failed its pre-registered gate. Do not tune the Phase 3 transform or
lower its support threshold. Test a new structural hypothesis instead.

Hypothesis:
A horse's Top-3 marginal probability is suitable for estimating inclusion in
the placings but is not necessarily a suitable first-place strength. Use role-
specific information:
- first-place probability: the already available normalized historical final
  win-market probability, identical for baseline and challenger;
- second/third conditional strength among remaining runners: frozen Phase 2
  Top-3 marginal q transformed as q/(1-q).

For each permutation i->j->k, compute:
P(i first from win market) *
P(j second from remaining Top-3 strengths) *
P(k third from remaining Top-3 strengths).
The unordered trio probability proxy is again the sum of all six permutations.

Freeze all Phase 2 model features/hyperparameters and the existing longshot
proxy boundary (historical final win odds >= 6.0). Do not introduce a new
threshold, model fit, odds segment, or staking rule.

Evaluate 2023 and 2024 reused development evidence with the same Phase 3
criteria:
- lower trifecta-proxy NLL and trio-proxy NLL;
- paired race bootstrap 1000 samples, seed 20261006;
- >=80% support for improvement;
- both overall and realized-top-three-containing-longshot segments;
- both years must pass.

Phase 4 does not require Phase 3 to pass; it is a replacement structural
hypothesis prompted by the Phase 3 failure, not threshold tuning.
Historical final win odds remain development-only proxies. No betting EV claim
is allowed without timestamped pre-race exotic-combination odds.
2025-2026 remain untouched. Forward Paper remains unchanged. No paid tool,
API, or data source may be used.

Phase 5 exact-three inclusion hypothesis:
Phase 4 also failed its pre-registered gate. Do not tune its thresholds or
weights. Test a different structural interpretation of the frozen Top-3
marginal probabilities.

Hypothesis:
The frozen Top-3 model estimates inclusion in the top three, so first construct
a probability distribution over unordered sets of exactly three runners.
For each horse use inclusion odds w=q/(1-q). For every size-three set S,
P(S | exactly 3) is proportional to the product of the three inclusion odds.
Normalize across all size-three sets exactly (implemented with the third
elementary symmetric polynomial; no fitting or Monte Carlo approximation).

For the realized three-runner set:
- trio probability proxy = the exact conditioned set probability;
- trifecta probability proxy = trio set probability multiplied by an order
  probability within those three runners derived only from the same historical
  win-market strengths for baseline and challenger.

This explicitly separates "which three horses make the placings" from "how
those three are ordered". It is a replacement hypothesis after Phase 3/4
failure, not a retune of either failed transform.

Freeze Phase 2 model features/hyperparameters, longshot boundary, and bootstrap
settings. Evaluate 2023 and 2024 reused development evidence, overall and
realized-top-three-containing-longshot segments. Require lower trifecta/trio
NLL and >=80% paired-race-bootstrap improvement support in both segments and
both years. Phase 3 and Phase 4 do not need to pass.

No EV claim or real ticket generation is allowed without timestamped pre-race
exotic-combination odds. 2025-2026 remain untouched. Forward Paper remains
unchanged. No paid tool, API, or data source may be used.
