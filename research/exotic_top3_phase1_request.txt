Research-only Exotic / Top-3 Phase 1 request.

Hypothesis:
Popular/non-popular runners may be mispriced for place-in-the-top-three outcomes
when past-only horse form, jockey history, running-style context, and the
composition of the current field are evaluated jointly rather than runner by
runner.

Design:
- Build a baseline Top-3 probability model using the existing pre-race v10
  history features plus existing win-market context.
- Build one challenger with fixed runner-vs-field interaction features:
  within-race means, deltas, dispersion, ranks, and market concentration.
- Use the same fixed CatBoost classifier settings for baseline and challenger.
- Train through 2022-12-31.
- Evaluate unchanged on reused development years 2023 and 2024.
- Report both overall Top-3 binary log-loss/Brier and the pre-registered
  historical-final-win-odds >= 6.0 longshot proxy.
- The development gate passes only if challenger binary log-loss and Brier are
  both lower than baseline overall AND in the longshot proxy in BOTH 2023 and
  2024.
- Do not tune a probability threshold, odds band, feature threshold, or staking
  rule in this phase.

Pedigree:
The current free history exposes a blood registration identifier but not
semantic sire/dam/broodmare-sire fields. Do not use the identifier as a
categorical memorization proxy. Pedigree features require a separately verified
free/legal semantic source before they may enter a later research phase.

Boundaries:
- 2023/2024 are reused development evidence, not independent holdout.
- 2025-2026 remain untouched as the final independent holdout.
- Do not read or evaluate 2025-2026.
- Forward Paper settings remain unchanged.
- Do not create or recommend real trifecta/trio tickets in this phase.
- Current free pipeline has no pre-race exotic-combination odds, so this phase
  tests probability quality only, not exotic-bet expected value.
- No paid tool, API, or data source may be used.
