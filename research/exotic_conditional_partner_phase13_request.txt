Phase 13 preregistration: conditional partner selection around a longshot anchor

Status: preregistered only. Do not run on real 2023/2024 development history until
the independently acquired provider-bounded pre-2025 JRA-VAN archive is complete,
its provenance is verified, and the one-shot execution receipt path is ready.

Structural hypothesis
---------------------
Earlier exotic phases failed when horse-level Top-3 marginals were mechanically
converted into trio/trifecta joint probabilities. Phase 13 does not estimate a
full three-horse ticket probability.

Instead:
1. Use the frozen Phase 2-style general Top-3 model to nominate one longshot
   anchor per race from the development-only historical-final-win-odds >= 6.0
   candidate set.
2. Conditional on that nominated anchor actually finishing Top 3, rank every
   other runner as a potential partner.
3. Select exactly two partners.
4. Compare the two selected partners with the two actual other Top-3 horses.

This tests whether the system can learn "who runs with the longshot" rather than
forcing independent marginal probabilities into a joint-distribution formula.

Frozen anchor rule
------------------
- Longshot candidate proxy: historical final win odds >= 6.0.
- Anchor: candidate with the highest frozen general Top-3 probability.
- No target/outcome information may participate in anchor nomination.
- Training anchors must use annual out-of-fold general-model probabilities for
  2018-2022. Same-row in-sample general-model predictions are forbidden.
- Evaluation anchors use the general model trained through 2022.
- Races without a longshot candidate are excluded from this conditional study.
- Partner-quality metrics are conditional on the nominated anchor actually
  finishing Top 3. Anchor hit rate is reported separately and cannot be hidden.

Frozen partner model
--------------------
- Objective: YetiRankPairwise.
- iterations=300
- depth=7
- learning_rate=0.05
- random_seed=42
- l2_leaf_reg remains the repository default (5.0).
- Candidate base features: exactly the frozen Phase 2-style general Top-3
  feature set (v10 past-only features + market context + runner-vs-field
  interactions).
- Add only fixed anchor-relative numeric context:
  partner_general_probability
  anchor_general_probability
  partner_vs_anchor_general_probability
  partner_market_implied_probability
  anchor_market_implied_probability
  partner_vs_anchor_market_probability
  partner_market_rank_minus_anchor
  and candidate-minus-anchor deltas for:
    horse_recent_top3_rate_5
    horse_recent_finish_percentile_mean_5
    horse_recent_early_ratio_mean_5
    horse_recent_late_ratio_mean_5
    jockey_past_win_rate
    jockey_course_past_win_rate
    horse_jockey_past_win_rate
    trainer_past_win_rate
- Raw horse IDs, blood-registration IDs, breeding-registration IDs, anchor IDs,
  finish positions, final payouts, and post-race values are forbidden as model
  features.

Training protocol
-----------------
- Build general pre-race features chronologically from independently acquired
  pre-2025 history.
- Annual OOF anchor folds: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022.
- For each fold, train the general Top-3 model only on prior dates and nominate
  anchors in that fold from OOF probabilities.
- Partner-ranker training rows come only from races in which the OOF-nominated
  anchor actually finished Top 3 and the race has exactly three Top-3 finishers.
- For each such race, every non-anchor runner is one ranking candidate; the two
  actual other Top-3 runners have target=1 and all others target=0.
- No 2023/2024 information may enter partner-ranker training.

Evaluation protocol
-------------------
Evaluate 2023 and 2024 separately with no retuning.

Report for each year:
- races_with_longshot_anchor_candidate
- nominated_anchor_top3_hit_rate
- conditional_partner_races
- baseline_exact_partner_pair_hit_rate
- challenger_exact_partner_pair_hit_rate
- exact_partner_pair_hit_rate_delta
- baseline_partner_recall_at_2
- challenger_partner_recall_at_2
- partner_recall_at_2_delta
- paired race bootstrap support, 1000 samples, seed 20261006.

Baseline partner selection:
- among non-anchor runners, choose the two highest frozen general Top-3
  probabilities.

Challenger partner selection:
- choose the two highest Phase 13 conditional partner-ranker scores.

Phase 13 gate
-------------
Both 2023 and 2024 must satisfy:
- exact partner-pair hit-rate delta > 0
- partner Recall@2 delta > 0
- bootstrap support >= 0.80 for both metrics.

No ranking metric may rescue a failed year, and no threshold/hyperparameter
retuning is allowed after seeing 2023/2024 results.

Interpretation boundary
-----------------------
Passing Phase 13 would mean only that conditional partner selection improved.
It does NOT establish:
- positive betting expected value,
- calibrated trio/trifecta ticket probability,
- profitable stake sizing,
- deployable live tickets.

Strict EV still requires lawful timestamped purchase-time trio/trifecta odds.
Historical O5/O6 final odds and final payouts remain forbidden as prebet price
evidence.

Safety
------
- 2025-2026 final independent holdout remains untouched.
- Forward Paper settings remain unchanged.
- Paid data/tools are not used.
- Real-ticket generation remains disabled.
- One real Phase 13 2023/2024 evaluation only; write an immutable local receipt
  after first successful evaluation.
