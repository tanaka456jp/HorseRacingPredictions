Research-only Exotic Longshot Rolling Residual Phase 10 request.

Observed Phase 9 evidence (reused development only):
- All four metrics moved in the favorable direction in BOTH 2023 and 2024.
- 2023: log-loss delta -0.00015949 (91.5% support), Brier -0.00003544
  (87.8%), top1 hit-rate +0.002606 (77.2%), MRR +0.001094 (75.2%).
- 2024: log-loss delta -0.00014286 (87.1% support), Brier essentially flat
  at -0.000000397 (50.8%), top1 +0.000290 (51.7%), MRR +0.000197 (53.9%).
- The strict Phase 9 gate therefore failed.
- Do NOT tune the residual scale, clipping, thresholds, odds bands, model depth,
  learning rate, or support threshold from these results.

Phase 10 structural hypothesis:
The residual relationship is directionally useful but drifts over time.
Use annual walk-forward refitting, not parameter tuning.

Fixed walk-forward protocol:
- OOF residual source years are 2018-2023.
- Every OOF residual target for year Y is generated by a general Top-3 model
  trained only on dates strictly before year Y.
- 2023 evaluation:
  - general baseline trained through 2022-12-31;
  - residual overlay trained on annual OOF longshot residuals from 2018-2022.
- 2024 evaluation:
  - general baseline trained through 2023-12-31;
  - residual overlay trained on annual OOF longshot residuals from 2018-2023;
  - critically, the 2023 residual target is generated from a baseline trained
    only through 2022, so 2024 training remains walk-forward safe.

Everything else stays frozen from Phase 9:
- longshot development proxy: historical final win odds >= 6.0;
- Phase 2-style general Top-3 feature/model family;
- Phase 7 specialist + residual overlay features;
- CatBoostRegressor RMSE, 250 iterations, depth 6, learning rate 0.03,
  random seed 42, l2_leaf_reg 8;
- predicted residual clip [-0.5, 0.5];
- corrected probability = clip(general probability + predicted residual,
  1e-6, 1-1e-6);
- no blend weight or calibration parameter.

Evaluation and strict gate:
Evaluate reused 2023 and 2024 separately against each year's matching rolling
general baseline. In BOTH years require:
- binary Top-3 log-loss delta < 0 and bootstrap support >= 80%;
- Brier delta < 0 and bootstrap support >= 80%;
- top1 longshot Top-3 hit-rate delta > 0 and support >= 80%;
- MRR delta > 0 and support >= 80%.
Paired race bootstrap remains 1000 samples, seed 20261006.

If Phase 10 fails, do not tune on 2023/2024. Use the failure pattern to choose
a new structural hypothesis.

Boundaries:
